# Permit Review Assistant: AI for Catching Incomplete Permit Applications Earlier

Client: City of San José, Permit Review
Team: Belinda Flores, Alondra Campos, Ivan barrera Rivera, Luis Garcia, Aug–Oct 2026
My part: I designed the applicant and City worker portals, directed the prototype build in Replit, and wrote the governance and user analysis.

## The problem
Permit applicants and permit staff both lose time when applications arrive incomplete. Staff review each one by hand, so missing documents are found late, feedback is slow, and housing projects wait.

## What we built
- An intake form runs an AI completeness check that flags missing documents and suggests where to route each application.
- Applicants see their projects, document receipt and a status timeline. City staff see a review queue with AI flags, routing actions, notes and analytics.
- The AI is advisory only. Authorized staff approve, deny or request revisions on every application.

## What I would do next
Feedback from Yu Chen (Sep 29) pointed to two gaps. Contractors with several projects have to re-enter the same details on every application, and the City already has a dashboard but no clear view of where applicants get stuck.
Next, I would add autofill for repeat contractor details, and use the AI results to show the City which mistakes happen most often so they can fix those steps.
