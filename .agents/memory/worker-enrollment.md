---
name: Worker enrollment boundary
description: Why staff permissions cannot be granted through public account registration
---

Newly registered accounts must remain applicants until an authorized operator has verified the person independently and explicitly granted worker access. Do not turn the public sign-up flow, a browser role choice, or unverified email domain into worker enrollment.

**Why:** This is a student prototype, not a County identity provider; public registration proves account control, not County employment or authorization. A mistaken worker grant exposes every applicant's records and permits status changes.

**How to apply:** When adding staff onboarding or role-management UI, keep grants behind an independently authorized operator workflow and default to applicant permissions on uncertainty.
